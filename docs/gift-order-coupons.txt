SPECIAL EDITION ORDER FLOW

Packages and server-authoritative prices: src/lib/giftPackages.ts.
Minimum: 10 gifts in total; customers may mix packages.
Maximum: 100,000 gifts per request.
The cart is saved in sessionStorage for the current browser tab; phone numbers are not saved.
/api/gift-order accepts action=quote (no email) or action=submit.
Submission uses the established preorder receiver. Its product field contains every package, quantity, unit price, line total and coupon discount; its price field contains the final total.
Gold-price APIs and the general preorder endpoint are unchanged.

COUPONS
Set GIFT_ORDER_COUPONS in Vercel for the intended deployment environment, then redeploy.
It is a JSON array of server-only rules. No codes are active by default.
Each rule uses code plus exactly one of percentOff (0 < value <= 100) or amountOff (positive integer MNT, applied to the whole order).
Optional fields: expiresAt (ISO timestamp, explicit timezone recommended) and minQuantity (integer).
Codes are case-insensitive. Discounts are rounded to whole MNT and cannot exceed the subtotal.
Codes are revalidated on submission; editing the cart requires the customer to reapply the code.
The owner must supply approved real codes and amounts before activation.
Coupons are reusable; per-customer or total usage limits are not implemented.
